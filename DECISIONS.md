Defaults chosen without asking, per standing rules. Newest first.

- 2026-07-26: Teacher and companion calls skip the scenario-only nightly agenda lookup. Reason: those persona IDs have no agenda rows, so every classroom opening paid for a 404 before using its built-in curriculum anyway.
- 2026-07-26: Persist the currently selected language when onboarding is completed, even if the learner accepts the default Kannada choice without tapping it first. Reason: production browser verification showed the default choice otherwise returned to onboarding after reload.
- 2026-07-26: A lesson coach may call the final check complete only when the exact start marker, three learner replies, and exact completion marker appear in the transcript. Reason: a production report incorrectly inferred completion from one corrected retry until this evidence rule was added.
- 2026-07-26: Keep `gemini-flash-lite-latest` with no `thinking_config`, and do not ship the faster pinned-model experiment. Reason: the verified production contract in this audit explicitly forbids model-specific thinking parameters on a mutable alias.
- 2026-07-26: Keep Saarika v2.5 at `language="unknown"` for every lesson, companion, and scenario call. Reason: English questions and code-mixed beginner speech must remain intelligible, and one misclassified synthetic probe is not grounds to override the verified real-speech behavior.
- 2026-07-26: Do not restore a canned acknowledgment to mask latency. Reason: five completed final-pipeline turns still miss the 1.5 second budget, but a fast fake response would hide brain failures and sacrifice conversational correctness.
- 2026-07-26: Reject unknown and malformed persona IDs at both `/api/token` and the worker, while allowing explicit `-d1`, `-d2`, and `-d3` scenario stages. Reason: silent fallback puts a learner into the wrong person and makes broken routes look healthy.
- 2026-07-26: Difficulty progresses from supported to everyday to challenge after 6 and 12 completed lessons, and the selected stage travels in the LiveKit room name. Reason: this adds useful scenario progression with existing completion data and no new service.
- 2026-07-26: Lesson completion now requires the exact final-check marker plus at least three learner attempts and two target-language attempts. Reason: an agent marker or a hangup alone is not evidence of a genuine speaking check.
- 2026-07-26: Keep complete, segment-ID-deduplicated transcripts in arrival order, and serialize Sarvam transliteration before display. Reason: the previous capped asynchronous caption path could reorder or lose turns and briefly expose native characters.

- 2026-07-26: Use Bulbul v3 MP3 streaming with a 30-character buffer and 50-character maximum chunk, shorter endpointing, preemptive LLM plus TTS, and explicit VAD interruption settings. Reason: these reduce avoidable delay with the existing providers, while retaining the supported audio codec and full semantic reply.
- 2026-07-13: Next.js keeps its current 16.2.10 release, with only its nested PostCSS dependency overridden to 8.5.10. Reason: this clears the active moderate advisory without accepting npm's unsafe forced downgrade to Next 9, and the production build passes with the patched dependency.
- 2026-07-13: Teachers are explicitly told that native characters in a learner turn come from speech recognition, never from learner typing. Reason: live Tamil and Hindi probes showed the teacher could otherwise scold a speaking student for the alphabet chosen by the STT service.
- 2026-07-13: Maatu now opens on Learn and treats the 15-lesson foundation course as the primary beginner path, while the Bazaar is labeled Practice. Reason: complete beginners need a recommended next step before they are asked to improvise with a character.
- 2026-07-13: Every lesson has a preview and an explicit passed or paused result, and course progress appears separately from real conversation history. Reason: starting or ending a call is not evidence of learning, while a visible speaking check makes the teacher's judgment understandable.
- 2026-07-13: The slow-down control now sends a reliable LiveKit control packet, lowers Sarvam TTS pace for the rest of the call, repeats the current point, and confirms the change back to the browser. Reason: a beginner support control must change the actual lesson, not only its button color.
- 2026-07-13: Course language switching lives inside Learn, captions and reduced-motion choices persist, native-script decoration is removed from the interface, and fonts are self-hosted by Next.js. Reason: these remove setup friction, honor the spoken-only rule, and keep the installed PWA visually stable offline.
- 2026-07-12: Agent-local start-time visit memory is retired; assessed completion in the app is the course record, and open tutoring receives the full curriculum reference. Reason: a started or probed call is not proof that a lesson was learned.
- 2026-07-12: Classroom lessons count as complete only when the teacher says the exact spoken assessment marker "Lesson complete" after the final check. Reason: hanging up is not evidence that a beginner mastered the chapter.
- 2026-07-12: The open Classroom session is "Ask your teacher", not another Conversation Mode. Reason: scenario calls already cover conversation, while Classroom must answer language questions and proactively teach or review.
- 2026-07-12: Nightly agenda learning is scoped to the exact persona ID, and a previously contaminated agenda is restored to that persona's default when it has no matching sessions. Reason: language-wide feedback copied auto-rickshaw goals into unrelated Kannada characters and was not honest self-improvement.
- 2026-07-12: Per-turn latency is logged once, at the first agent audio after a learner speech end, and repeated VAD transitions are ignored while the acknowledgment and reply are active. Reason: the acceptance metric is first audio, and duplicate lines made one turn look both inside and outside budget.
- 2026-07-12: Street and Progress stats are scoped by a per-device anonymous ID and aggregated from Neon session rows; a new device gets an explicit zero state. Reason: this removes all demo progress without adding signup friction.
- 2026-07-12: Live learner captions keep Saarika v2.5 for speech recognition, then use Sarvam Transliterate for Latin-script display and persistence. Reason: Saarika v2.5 does not support translit mode directly, and the server route keeps the Sarvam key private.
- 2026-07-12: PWA installability uses a small first-party service worker with an offline shell and strict service-worker headers. Reason: the existing manifest and icons were valid, but production had no service worker.
- 2026-07-12: The nightly self-improving loop runs as a Vercel Cron at 02:00 IST, stores versioned before and after agendas in Neon, and the Mac voice worker fetches the active agenda when each call starts. Reason: Vercel provides a reliable nightly scheduler but cannot persistently edit repository JSON, while launchd cannot read the repo because of macOS TCC; Neon is the durable bridge and keeps every rewrite auditable.
- 2026-07-12: The LaunchAgent runs LiveKit jobs in the worker process with the thread executor, disables dev auto-reload, pre-generates LLM plus TTS output, and gives a short language-specific spoken acknowledgment at speech end. Reason: macOS background execution repeatedly timed out LiveKit's spawned job process before the persona loaded; thread execution removes that failing process boundary, and the natural acknowledgment bridges the full reply while keeping agent-audio-start inside the 1.5 second budget.

## 2026-07-17 learning audit repairs
- Added Lesson 16 instead of stretching existing lessons: the scenario personas demand tens, rupees, haggling, directions and rescue phrases, and a single survival-kit lesson closes that gap without reshuffling the arc learners may already be inside.
- Taught Tamil "because" as adhunaala (that is why) rather than yennaa: yennaa collides with enna (what) for a beginner listening by ear.
- Lesson pass check now merges the raw transcript with the romanized one: the "Lesson complete" marker must never be destroyed by a romanization API failure. Raw text stays out of the UI; it is only scanned for the marker.
- Coach report fields are transliterated server side with a strip fallback: the romanized-UI guarantee now covers Gemini output, not just captions.
- The agent launcher wraps the worker in caffeinate -s: this Mac sleeps after 1 minute and only audio assertions were keeping the school online.
- Left the open /api/token and /api/report GET routes as flagged risks, not fixed: adding auth would break the public PWA and the spend risk is small at current scale; revisit before any promotion push.
- 2026-07-22: Classroom went silent in production: Google repointed the gemini-flash-lite-latest alias to a model that rejects thinking_budget 0, so EVERY brain call returned 400 INVALID_ARGUMENT and the learner only ever heard the canned one-word acknowledgment (Sari/Theek hai/Seri). Fix: removed thinking_config from the Gemini brain (new lite model spends zero thought tokens on short turns anyway, ~1s replies, verified through the livekit plugin). Also removed the canned acknowledgment machinery entirely: when the brain broke it masked the failure, and even healthy it made the teacher sound robotic; preemptive_generation already covers latency.
- 2026-07-26: Root cause of "I cannot actually converse or learn": the STT was language-locked to the target language, so a beginner's ENGLISH speech was transcribed as phonetic native script ("what does namaskara mean" -> Kannada letters). Proven against the live Sarvam API. Switched STT to language="unknown" (auto-detect): verified it transcribes pure kn/hi/ta identically to the locked mode, and returns clean romanized text for code-mixed speech; the streaming WebSocket accepts language-code=unknown exactly like the locked codes. Deleted the two prompt hacks that told the teacher to "sound out" native-script English, since they are now obsolete and would mislead the model.
- 2026-07-26: Scenarios expanded 6 -> 14 (42 personas) to cover everyday Indian life as requested: added vegetable market, kirana provision store, clinic/doctor, local eatery, neighbour, landlord, salon/barber, pharmacy. Ordered everyday-first on the hub. Auto rickshaw and delivery rider (the Swiggy-style gate call) already existed.
- 2026-07-26: Free conversation was buried inside the Classroom screen, so it was effectively undiscoverable; the open companion (chat about anything, teach any topic on request, act out any invented scene) already existed but was never found. Promoted it to a primary card on the hub and made it return to whichever screen launched it.
