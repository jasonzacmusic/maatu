
# Hindi

## Situations

### Auto and cab (talking to: Driver)
- THEM aap kahaan jaa rahe hain?   = Where are you going?
- YOU  main station jaa raha hoon   = I'm going to the station.
- YOU  kiraaya kitna hai?   = How much is the fare?
- YOU  seedha jaaiye   = Please go straight.
- YOU  left mudiye   = Please turn left.
- YOU  dheere chalaaiye   = Please drive slowly.
- YOU  yahaan rokiye   = Please stop here.
- THEM kya aapke paas chhutta hai?   = Do you have change?
- YOU  mere paas chhutta nahin hai   = I do not have change.
- YOU  main cash pay karunga   = I'll pay cash.

### Passport office (talking to: Officer)
- THEM token dikhaaiye   = Please show your token.
- YOU  main passport renew karna chaahta hoon   = I want to renew the passport.
- THEM form bhariye   = Please fill your form.
- THEM yahaan sign kijiye   = Please sign here.
- THEM kya aapke paas Aadhaar card hai?   = Do you have your Aadhaar card?
- YOU  mere paas Aadhaar card hai   = I have my Aadhaar card.
- THEM mujhe address proof chaahiye   = I need address proof.
- YOU  fees kitni hai?   = How much are the fees?
- THEM wahaan wait kijiye   = Please wait there.
- THEM kal vaapas aaiye   = Please come back tomorrow.

### Doctor and hospital (talking to: Doctor)
- YOU  mujhe bukhaar hai   = I have a fever.
- YOU  mujhe sir dard hai   = I have a headache.
- THEM kya aapko khaansi hai?   = Do you have a cough?
- YOU  mujhe khaansi nahin hai   = I do not have a cough.
- YOU  mujhe sugar hai   = I have diabetes.
- THEM report dikhaaiye   = Please show the report.
- THEM goli lijiye   = Please take the tablet.
- THEM bahut paani pijiye   = Please drink a lot of water.
- THEM cheeni mat khaaiye   = Please do not eat sugar.
- THEM aaraam kijiye   = Please rest.
- YOU  medical store kahaan hai?   = Where is the pharmacy?

### Medical shop (talking to: Chemist)
