# Maatu spoken studio direction contract

User intent: teach colloquial Tamil, Kannada, Hindi and French through ordinary conversations, authentic scenarios, and a reusable algorithm for building sentences. Deploy the finished public site. Canada is interpreted as Kannada because the existing repository is a Kannada learning product.

Direction: neighborhood conversation studio. Warm daylight paper, generous but useful space, Newsreader display type, clear Hanken Grotesk controls, conversational purple, quiet mint and peach. Actual documented city photography carries place. Photographs illustrate local life, never portray the fictional AI characters as real people.

Signature: five connected colored sentence stops, Who / Does / What / Describe / When, with the same colors labeling the sentence. The noun, verb and person remain while language and tense change. Gender, particles, articles and word order follow the chosen language.

First viewport: all four languages are visible; Just talk, Real-life scenes and Sentence lab stay one click away. A conversation invitation and an authentic everyday city photo sit beside each other on desktop and stack on mobile. Both voice and a typed first thought start a real conversation. No synthetic testimonials, progress, online status or metrics.
