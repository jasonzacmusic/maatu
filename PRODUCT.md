# Maatu

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Adults learning to speak everyday Tamil, Kannada, Hindi, or French. Beginners can use English for support. Music teaching remains a supported topic, alongside any topic the learner chooses.

## Product Purpose
Keep a natural conversation going while helping the learner say more in the target language. Three equal entry points: open conversation, authentic scenarios, and a reusable sentence flowchart.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Next.js, LiveKit Cloud, Gemini Live speech to speech, Neon reports, and an installable PWA. Pronunciation follows Chennai Tamil, Bengaluru Kannada, Delhi Hindi, and Paris French. Latin captions support speaking without requiring Indian scripts. Real progress comes from actual sessions or assessed lessons. Never infer mastery from starting a call.

## Brand Commitments
Maatu is a Nathaniel School of Music project by Jason Zac. Colloquial, friendly, practical speech. No em dashes. Preserve the 16-lesson course and saved practice lines.

## Product Principles
- Teach within the learner's topic instead of resetting the conversation.
- Answer meaning questions before requesting repetition.
- Accept close attempts; give one correction and one retry for meaningful mistakes.
- Use the same sentence choices across languages while respecting their grammar.
- Represent everyday settings with language-specific places, people, and details.

## Evidence on Hand
grammar.json, curriculum.json, playbooks.json, and the scenario personas are the existing teaching data. French parity is part of this redesign. No testimonials or invented progress.

## Open Decisions
Daylight conversation studio is the selected working direction. Verified everyday city photographs replace the unavailable generated illustration. The interpretation of Canada as Kannada follows the existing product and is stated to the user.
