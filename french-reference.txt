SPOKEN FRENCH REFERENCE. Same deterministic forms as Maatu's sentence flowchart.
Persons: je (I), tu (friend), vous (polite), il, elle, on (we), ils. French does not share Indian-language word order. Keep articles, contractions and gender agreement. On uses a singular verb; plural participles can refer to the group. Negative speech drops ne. Near future uses aller plus infinitive. Questions can keep normal word order with rising intonation.
