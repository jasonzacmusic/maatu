## 2. What we teach

**Instruments**

- Piano and keyboard
- Guitar: acoustic and electric
- Vocals: Western vocal training, across many styles
- Bass guitar

**Musicianship** (the skills behind every instrument)

- Music theory
- Ear training
- Rhythm
- Reading music and sight reading
- Transcription, which means working out a song by ear and writing it down
- Improvisation and composition

**How we teach, in plain words**

- **Harmony and rhythm first.** Many schools start with the melody in the right hand. We start from the bottom up. First we build chords and rhythm. Then you use them to play any melody, and later to make your own music.
- **Technique, theory and ear together.** We call this our 3D approach. In one lesson you train your hands, your understanding and your ears at the same time.
- **The student comes first.** Teachers look at what each student needs and build exercises for it.
- **Many styles.** Students explore rock, pop, blues, gospel, jazz, Bollywood, worship and folk music, with ideas from Indian music too.
- **Rhythm is taken seriously.** We teach rhythm as a full subject, including Konnakol (South Indian spoken rhythm), Afro-Cuban rhythms, and odd time signatures like 7/8.
- **A certificate.** After each semester you receive a graded, performance-based certificate.
- **Exam preparation.** We are not part of any exam board, such as Trinity or ABRSM. But our training lets you take those exams, with extra help from our teachers if you want it.

## 3. Ways to learn

**Music Method Foundation course**
This is our flagship course for beginners. It has 32 lessons within six months: 20 practical lessons on your instrument, about once a week, and 12 musicianship lessons on theory, ear training and rhythm, about every other week. It is taught in small groups, for piano, guitar or vocals. You receive a certificate at the end.

**Intermediate and Advanced classrooms**
These are for students who know the basics. You choose classrooms like school subjects: Modular Piano (short topic modules), Music Gym (technique, theory and ear exercises), Theory and Ear Training, and Music Factory (learning whole songs by ear and writing them down). A plan is counted in hours over six months, and you choose the classrooms.

**Private lessons**
This is one teacher and one student. Timing is flexible, and the lessons follow your goals. Private lessons can be combined with group theory classes. For younger children, private lessons of 30 to 45 minutes are an option.

**Public classes**
A public class is one live class on one topic, on Zoom. It is open to anyone. You pay once, with no subscription. The Zoom link comes by email, and the time is shown in your own time zone.

**Workshops with Jason Zac**
Jason Zac runs special workshops. Through the year we also hold genre, artist and seasonal workshops, a good way to meet our community.

**Doubt clearing**
Stuck on something? You record a short video question and play the part that is not working. Jason Zac watches it and answers with his own video, within 48 hours. You pay once for a set number of answers. Nothing renews on its own. Each question can be public or private.

**Everything for Life (self-paced courses)**
Our self-paced video courses are at https://learn-music.nathanielschool.com. You pay once and keep access for life. There are more than 160 video lessons in piano, ear training, music theory, reading music and singing while playing, plus song collections like The Beatles and Ilaiyaraaja.

**Patreon resources**
Jason Zac's Patreon, https://www.patreon.com/jasonzac, is a monthly membership. It unlocks the files behind each YouTube lesson, like PDFs and MIDI files. It is a resource library, not a structured course.

**Free lessons**
Our free lessons website, https://free-music-lessons.nathanielschool.com, has thousands of free video lessons, sorted by topic and skill level. There is no paywall.

**Free workshop**
"An Introduction to Music" is a free live online workshop with Jason Zac: rhythm, melody and harmony, in 75 minutes plus questions. It is for all ages. You need no instrument and no music background. The next date is still being announced. Sign up at https://workshop.nathanielschool.com.

**Free practice apps**
We build free music practice apps that run in your web browser. There is nothing to install. You can find them all at https://apps.nathanielschool.com. See section 5.

## 4. How classes work

1. **Joining class.** You log in to the Learning Portal at https://learning-portal.nathanielschool.com. Your class appears on your dashboard. You press "Join", and the Zoom class opens.
2. **The live class.** A teacher teaches you live. It is a real class, not a pre-recorded video.
3. **After class.** The class recording appears on your portal soon after the class ends. You can loop a hard part and slow it down to practise.
4. **Notes and homework.** Revision notes, assignments and weekly homework appear on your dashboard.
5. **Feedback.** You record yourself playing the homework and send it in. Your teacher watches it and writes back with notes on your playing.
6. **Certificate.** At the end of the semester you receive your certificate.

**How often?** In the Music Method you have one instrument or vocal lesson each week, and theory and ear training every other week. Electives like Music Gym and Music Factory add more classes.

**Class times.** Most classes run in the early morning or evening, India time, and there are weekend batches. Timings change, so the course advisor confirms current times.

- For example, Music Factory has met on Sunday evenings at 5:45 pm India time.
- For example, Music Gym has met early on Saturday mornings, around 6:30 am India time.
- For example, an online piano Foundation batch started on 3 September 2026 with piano on Thursdays at 8 pm, and theory with Jason Zac on alternate Saturdays at 7:45 am, India time.
- For example, public classes have been repeated on Sundays at 6:30 pm India time for students in other countries.

**Before your first online class.** Set up your camera so the teacher can see your hands and hear your instrument clearly. Our free setup guide is at https://studio.nathanielschool.com/obs-guide.

## 7. Frequently asked questions

**I am an adult and a complete beginner. Can I join?**
Yes. Our Foundation course is made for complete beginners. There are only two requirements: you love music, and you want to learn.

**Can children join?**
Yes. Our students range from age 7 to 75 and above. Our six-month Music Method course is designed for children aged 8 and above. Younger children can take private lessons of 30 to 45 minutes.

**Which instrument should I learn first?**
Our course advisor can help with that on WhatsApp at +91 77604 56847. If you are not sure yet, try our free workshop. You do not need an instrument for it.

**Can I learn online from outside India?**
Yes. We have students in Singapore, Europe, the US, the UK, the UAE and many other places. Classes are live on Zoom, and every class is recorded.

**Do you offer a trial class or a demo?**
No, we do not offer free demo classes. You can watch our YouTube lessons, use our free lessons website, or join our free workshop to see how we teach.

**What if I miss a class?**
Every online class is recorded, and the recording, notes and homework go to your portal. Group classes have no separate make-up class. If you tell your teacher in advance, we can try to move the lesson. Private lessons are easier to reschedule.

**How long is a class?**
Most classes are 60 minutes. Music Gym is 45 minutes, and Music Factory is 90 minutes. Private lessons are 45 to 60 minutes.

**How long is the course?**
The Foundation course is 32 lessons within six months. Plans run for six months. After that you can continue with the next six-month cycle.

**Do I need a piano at home?**
Not on the first day. But you will need one at home for homework. We suggest a touch-sensitive keyboard with 61 to 88 keys, and we share a buying guide after you register.

**Do I need my own guitar?**
Yes. You need a basic acoustic guitar to practise every day. Our teachers can help you choose one.

**Do I get a certificate?**
Yes. You receive a graded, performance-based certificate after you complete your semester.

**Do you prepare students for Trinity or ABRSM exams?**
We are not part of any exam board. But our training prepares you to take those exams, and our teachers can help you get ready if that is your goal.

**Who are the teachers?**
The school is led by Jason Zac and Lillian Zachariah. Our teachers include Infant Kenneth and Dharan Prasad for piano, Prateek Sonteke for guitar, and Shami Mohan and Alan Thomas Sunish for vocals. Every teacher follows the same Nathaniel School Music Method.

**Can I learn with Jason Zac himself?**
Jason Zac teaches theory, Music Gym, Music Factory and a few piano batches, so his places are limited. Many students take theory with Jason and their instrument with another teacher.

**How big are the groups?**
Groups are small, so everyone gets attention. For example, guitar groups have 2 to 3 students, and vocal groups have 4 to 8.

**Group or private: which is better for a beginner?**
For a new beginner, we usually recommend group classes. Batchmates keep each other motivated, and you can move to private lessons later. Private lessons suit you if you need flexible timing or have a very specific goal.

**Can I bring my own songs?**
Yes, we encourage it. Bollywood, jazz, gospel, pop or classical: bring the music you love.

**I learned before, or I taught myself. Where do I start?**
Many self-taught players and grade-exam students start with Foundation to fill the gaps. If you already have strong basics, send us an audition clip. Jason Zac reviews it, and our advisor places you in the right class.

**What level will I reach in six months?**
We do not promise a level. Music is like a sport: progress depends on your passion and your practice. Our job is to keep helping you grow.

**How much should I practise?**
For singing and piano, we suggest at least 30 minutes a day, or 3 to 4 hours a week. For guitar, we suggest about one hour a day, or at least 5 hours a week.

**Can I pause my course?**
If you have a family emergency or a serious injury, we can move you into our next batch. You continue from where you left off.

**Can I visit the studio?**
Yes. Please message us first. Saturdays between 9:30 am and 5:30 pm are the best time to visit. We do not hold meetings on Sundays, because Sundays are for live lessons.

**Will I get to perform?**
Yes. Students perform at our school concerts, jam sessions and bootcamps. Online students can record their playing and share it with our community for feedback.

**What do I need for online classes?**
You need a stable internet connection, a phone, tablet or laptop with a camera and microphone, and your instrument. Place the camera so the teacher can see both your hands. Our setup guide is at https://studio.nathanielschool.com/obs-guide.

**Can I learn two instruments?**
Yes, we have a two-instrument option. Our course advisor can plan it with you.

**Do you teach Carnatic or Hindustani music?**
Our vocal course includes an introduction to ragas, and our lessons draw on Indian music. For a full Carnatic or Hindustani course, our course advisor can help with that on WhatsApp at +91 77604 56847.

**Do you give sheet music?**
We teach reading and use written music. When a piece needs notation, we often build it together in class. A lot of music is also learned by ear.

Our course advisor will share current fees on WhatsApp at +91 77604 56847.
